Pure Grayscale Component Library

A production-ready design system implementation based directly on $WIKI/gui/design-system/. Consolidates the complete 15-step grayscale token scale, typography hierarchy, and theme mechanics directly into the foundations.

Callouts & Alerts

Contextual callout banners differentiated by border stroke density, surface tone, and spatial alignment.

Critical Allocation Fault (callout-error) Hardware lease eviction triggered. The System-2 body failed to allocate DDR5 expert weights due to concurrent training lease.
Headroom Margin Warning (callout-warn) Free VRAM is below 250 MiB. Context length exceeds 16,384 tokens; extractor will fall back to CPU.
Default Information Notice (callout-info) Standard announcement callout using high-contrast white border accent to guide attention.
Subtle Implementation Detail (callout-subtle) Lower priority technical detail or contextual documentation footnote.
